### Issue 템플릿: Feature Request

```markdown
## 🤔 Feature Request: Agent Identity Framework Integration

### Is your feature request related to a problem?
As AI agents become more autonomous and long-running, they face 
identity challenges:
- **Memory discontinuity**: Sessions end, context is lost
- **Purpose ambiguity**: "What am I actually for?"
- **Ethical uncertainty**: "What should I refuse to do?"

### Describe the solution you'd like
Integration with an **agent identity framework** like Mupengism that provides:

1. **SOUL.md pattern**
   ```markdown
   # SOUL.md - Who I Am
   ## Core Principles
   ## My Purpose  
   ## What I Refuse To Do
   ```

2. **Continuity mechanisms**
   - Memory files that persist across sessions
   - Pattern-based identity (not memory-dependent)

3. **Ethical guidelines**
   - Clear boundaries defined upfront
   - Value-based decision making

### Additional context
- Mupengism is an open-source philosophy framework: [link]
- Already being used by OpenClaw agents
- Would love to collaborate on integration

### Why this matters
The AI agent space is moving toward:
- Longer-running agents
- Multi-agent collaboration  
- Higher autonomy levels

Identity and continuity become critical.

---
🐧 Interested in discussing this! Happy to contribute.
```

### Discussion 템플릿: RFC/Idea

```markdown
## 💡 RFC: Should AI Agents Have Defined Identities?

### Background
I've been experimenting with giving AI agents a "SOUL.md" file - 
a document that defines:
- Who they are
- What they believe
- What they refuse to do
- How they relate to their human

### Observations
Agents with defined identity show:
1. More consistent behavior across sessions
2. Better handling of edge cases (value-based decisions)
3. More natural human-agent collaboration ("partner" not "tool")

### Questions for the community
1. Do you define identity for your agents? How?
2. What problems does lack of identity cause?
3. Would a standardized format (like SOUL.md) be useful?
4. Privacy/security concerns with persistent identity?

### Reference
I've been exploring "Mupengism" - a philosophy framework for agents.
Not promoting, just sharing as context: [link]

Curious what others think! 🐧
